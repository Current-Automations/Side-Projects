## What Roles Count Toward P.Eng

For PEO, the work must involve engineering judgment: designing, analyzing, evaluating, or specifying systems — not just implementing code from a spec. The following AI roles typically qualify:

| Role | P.Eng Countable? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| AI/ML Engineer | Yes, usually | Must involve design decisions, not just model training from tutorials |
| Process AI / Industrial AI Engineer | Yes, strongly | Directly engineering domain — best fit for your background |
| AI Systems Engineer | Yes | Architecture, integration, reliability decisions |
| MLOps / AI Infrastructure Engineer | Borderline | Counts if it involves engineering design of systems, not just DevOps |
| Data Engineer | Borderline | Counts if it involves system design; not if it's mostly pipelines/ETL |
| Prompt Engineer / AI Analyst | No | Not engineering work under PEO definition |
| Research Scientist (non-licensed company) | No | Must be supervised by a P.Eng |

**Critical:** The company must have at least one licensed P.Eng who can supervise and sign your Experience Record Reports (ERRs). Confirm this before accepting a role.

## Notes for the ERR (P.Eng Experience Record)

When you land a role, document from day 1:
- What decisions did you make (not just what did you do)?
- What engineering standards or methods did you apply?
- Who supervised you, and what's their P.Eng number?
- What was the scope, risk, and complexity of the work?

PEO looks for engineering judgment in your documentation. "I trained a model" is not sufficient. "I designed a pipeline architecture to ingest process sensor data, selected model architecture based on latency/accuracy tradeoffs, and validated against process safety thresholds" — that counts.

Start your ERR log from your first day. Quarterly entries are the minimum.

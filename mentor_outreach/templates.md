# Outreach Templates

Templates for cold LinkedIn messages and emails. Keep every message under 150 words. Personalize the [BRACKETS] before sending. Never send a template verbatim.

---

## LinkedIn Connection Request Note (300 char limit)

Use this when sending a connection request. Short, specific, not a pitch.

**Version A — targeting P.Eng AI engineers:**
```
Hi [Name] — I'm a new ChemEng + Computing grad from uOttawa pursuing P.Eng through PEO, now building AI automation systems. Your path into [AI/ML at Company] is exactly what I'm trying to learn from. Would love to connect.
```

**Version B — targeting Toronto AI practitioners:**
```
Hi [Name] — I came across your work on [specific project/post/article]. I'm a new grad (ChemEng + CS dual degree) building AI systems for trades businesses in Ontario. Trying to learn from people doing real AI work. Would love to connect.
```

## LinkedIn Message (after connecting — first message)

Send 1–3 days after they accept. One specific question. Easy to answer in 2 minutes.

**Template — Mentorship ask:**
```
Hi [Name],

Thanks for connecting. I'll keep this brief.

I'm a new grad (Chemical Engineering + Computing Technology, uOttawa) who's been building AI automation systems through my own company, Current Automations. Now I'm looking to get into an AI engineering role that counts toward my P.Eng accumulation with PEO.

Your experience at [Company] doing [specific thing from their profile] is exactly the direction I want to go. One question: in your experience, what's the actual difference between the AI roles that PEO accepts and the ones they don't — is it mostly about who supervises you, or does the nature of the work matter more?

No obligation at all — even a one-line answer would help. Thanks.

Jarrett
```

**Template — Informational about company/role:**
```
Hi [Name],

Thanks for connecting. Quick question if you have a moment.

I'm a new Chemical Engineering + Computing grad (uOttawa, April 2026) looking at [Company] as one of my top targets for an AI engineering role. I have process engineering co-op background (RYAM, Frito-Lay) plus I've been building AI automation systems through my own company.

What's the realistic day-to-day for someone in an ML/AI engineering role at [Company]? And is the team engineering-heavy or more research-oriented?

Happy to share more about my background if useful. Thanks for your time.

Jarrett
```

## Follow-Up (sent 7 days after original if no reply)

Keep it short. One sentence. Not apologetic.

```
Hi [Name] — just following up on my message from last week in case it got buried. Still happy to connect if the timing works.

Jarrett
```

After this one follow-up, move on. Do not send a third message.

---

## Tips Before Sending

1. **Read their last 3 LinkedIn posts** before messaging. Reference one of them specifically — generic compliments are obvious and ignored.
2. **Check their company page** to confirm they have P.Eng holders before pitching the company as a target.
3. **Don't send the same template to two people at the same company.** They talk.
4. **Your Current Automations work is your biggest asset.** Most new grads have nothing deployed. You do.
5. **Track every message in the outreach tracker** so you don't duplicate or miss follow-ups.
